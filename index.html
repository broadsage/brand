Developer Platform Engineering (DPE) Architecture & Open-Source Mission

The Open-Source Way
Mission

Broadsage champions the open-source way by building transparent, community-driven technologies and transforming distributed open-source stacks—from agentic AI to cloud infrastructure—into hardened, enterprise-grade architectures that empower engineering freedom without vendor lock-in.

Vision

“Broad Vision, Sage Guidance — Open to Everyone.” To be the global catalyst and trusted foundation for open enterprise computing. By uniting radical open-source transparency with calm, authoritative architectural guidance, Broadsage ensures that the future of software engineering remains open, sovereign, and accessible to all developers.

Broadsage operates as a specialized Developer Platform Engineering (DPE) initiative. Rather than fragmenting into fictional commercial product silos, our identity serves as the strategic architectural conduit uniting fast-moving open-source innovation with hardened enterprise platform engineering under the core philosophy: “Broad Vision, Sage Guidance — Open to Everyone.”

The "X" Clearspace Rule

The exclusion zone guarantees that Ollie is never crowded by text, borders, or neighboring UI elements. The unit X is defined as 1/4th of the mascot height (or the exact height of Ollie's glasses).

  • 🔹 Digital Minimum (Emblem): 32px × 32px (Favicon / App Tray)
  • 🔹 Digital Minimum (Wordmark): 140px width × 32px height
  • 🔹 Print Minimum (Emblem): 12mm × 12mm
  • 🔹 Print Minimum (Wordmark): 35mm width

5. Strict Brand Usage Guidelines: Do's & Don'ts

Preserve visual integrity across all digital, print, and community applications

Brand Governance
✓ DO

Use Official SVGs Without Modification

Always render Ollie using original vector assets with correct proportional scaling and verified gradients.

✕ DON'T

Never Stretch or Distort Aspect Ratio

Do not skew, squash, or non-uniformly stretch the emblem. Scale with locked 1:1 aspect ratio at all times.

✓ DO
BROADSAGE

Maintain Two-Tone Wordmark Styling

Keep BROAD in Crisp White and SAGE in Solar Gold Amber to preserve the dual vision-guidance hierarchy.

✕ DON'T

Never Alter Official Brand Colors

Do not tint Ollie with pink, purple, or unapproved gradients. Only Palette 3 (Solar Gold & Midnight Navy) is authorized.

✓ DO
Dark Mode Native Light Mode Clear

Ensure High Background Contrast

Place Ollie on Midnight Navy, solid dark surfaces (#0A1128 / #050814), or pure Crisp White for maximum contrast.

✕ DON'T

Never Place on Busy or Clashing Canvas

Never superimpose the logo over cluttered photography, low-contrast yellow backgrounds, or complex gradients.
