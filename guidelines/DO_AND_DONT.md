# Broadsage Brand Governance: Do's & Don'ts

Maintaining visual consistency across our global developer ecosystem is vital to preserving trust, authority, and recognition. Follow these mandatory rules when utilizing Broadsage marks.

---

## 1. Clearspace & Geometry

### DO:
- **Maintain Minimum Clearspace:** Always preserve an exclusion margin of at least $X = 0.25 \times \text{Height}$ of the mascot on all four sides.
- **Scale Proportionately:** Always lock the 1:1 aspect ratio when resizing the square emblem or circular avatar.
- **Center Visual Weight:** When aligning Ollie with copy, align based on the optical center of the owl's body, not the bounding box ear tip.

### DON'T:
- **Never Stretch or Squash:** Non-uniform scaling distorts Ollie's eyeglasses and beacon shield.
- **Never Rotate or Tilt:** Ollie must sit upright with vertical alignment at all times.
- **Never Crop Internally:** Do not slice off Ollie's ears or beacon shield unless using the official circular avatar mask.

---

## 2. Color Integrity

### DO:
- **Use Palette 3 (Solar Gold & Midnight Navy):** Only official colors (`#0A1128`, `#F59E0B`, `#0284C7`, `#FFFFFF`) are approved.
- **Keep the Sage Beacon Amber:** Ollie’s belly shield must radiate Solar Gold Amber (`#F59E0B`). It represents the beacon of guidance.
- **Preserve Two-Tone Wordmark:** Keep `BROAD` in Crisp White and `SAGE` in Solar Gold Amber.

### DON'T:
- **Never Recolor with Unapproved Hues:** Do not color Ollie pink, purple, neon green, or pastels.
- **Never Invert Gradients Erroneously:** Do not apply inverted light-to-dark gradients that make the owl look hollow.
- **Never Use Low-Contrast Combinations:** Never place yellow text on a white canvas or dark navy text on a black canvas.

---

## 3. Backgrounds & Contrast

### DO:
- **Dark Mode Native Canvas:** Ollie was born in the dark; place him on Midnight Navy (`#0A1128`), Deep Obsidian (`#050814`), or dark terminal editors.
- **Light Mode Canvas:** For light decks or white paper, use `broadsage_logo_light.svg` or `broadsage_logo_horizontal_light.svg`.
- **Transparent Backdrops:** Ensure adequate background contrast when embedding on transparent navigation bars.

### DON'T:
- **Never Place on Busy Photos:** Avoid placing marks directly over intricate photographic textures or clashing patterns.
- **Never Apply Drop Shadow Clutter:** Do not add gaudy 3D drop shadows, bevels, or heavy neon outer glows.

---

## 4. Typography & Wordmark

### DO:
- **Use Approved Geometric Sans:** Wordmark uses heavy geometric grotesque with tight tracking (`-0.5px`).
- **Use Official Philosophy Subtitle:** The subtext `Broad Vision, Sage Guidance — Open to Everyone.` is set in Slate (`#94A3B8`) with balanced tracking.

### DON'T:
- **Never Substitute Novelty Fonts:** Never re-type the wordmark in decorative, handwritten, or serif typefaces.
- **Never Separate Letters:** Do not alter the relative scale between `BROAD` and `SAGE`.
